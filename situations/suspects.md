Suspect 1: The Victim's friend
The girl stated: "I was just visiting my friend. Why would I murder them? I have no motivation to do so. Sure, they're annoying at times. But I have no reason to do an evil thing like that! What do you take me for?"

Suspect 2: The Victim's Elderly Neighbor From Before They Moved In
The neighbor stated: "I just came to give them some of my tea. She came out of her room and said she was thankful for it. I never even stepped foot into her apartment. Plus, my back would give out if I tried to kill them."

Suspect 3: The Victim's Father
The father stated: "I came to visit my child. Is that so wrong of me? I only came to give them some of their childhood items, nothing more. How could I kill my own child that I raised?"
